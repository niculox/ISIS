## 🌌 Project Overview

**ISIS** (*Health Indicators Software Integration*) is a cognitive prosthesis and external executive support system developed for long-duration space missions (such as crewed missions to Mars or the Lunar Gateway). 

In deep exploration environments, the human body is not the only system subjected to severe stressors: **the brain is the primary line of wear and tear**. The NASA Human Research Roadmap demonstrates that astronauts face a perfect storm of adverse neurobehavioral factors: chronic sleep deprivation, extreme isolation, and decision fatigue compounded by communication lag with Earth. 

ISIS acts as a local support ecosystem that processes cognitive stress and reduces operational friction without relying on real-time connections to ground control.

---

## 🎯 The Real Problem in Orbit

According to the guidelines of the *NASA Human Research Roadmap* addressed in the project, the human brain on long-duration space missions suffers from critical pillars of degradation:

* **Chronic Sleep Deprivation:** An average of only **6 hours per night**, with roughly **19%** of rest periods circadically misaligned, generating attention deficits equivalent to a full night awake.
* **Communication Signal Delay:** A delay of **20 minutes each way** ($20\text{ min}$ round trip) between Mars and Earth, preventing real-time psychological or medical support.
* **Executive Paralysis and Overload:** Years away from Earth, prolonged isolation, and severe exhaustion of the prefrontal cortex, leading to operational failures and neurobehavioral breakdowns.

## 🧠 Neuroscience-Based Architecture

The core differentiator of ISIS is its **Brain-Based Interface Engineering**, structured upon three fundamental pillars present in its software architecture:

```
[ W3C COGA Principle ] ---> Cognitive Load Filter (1 action per screen / micro-steps)
[ Anti-Punishment Core ] -> Sustainable Dopamine (reporting malaise scores equally / no streak reset)
[ Transition Buffers ] ----> Transition Buffers (respecting neurobiological rhythms and micro-pausas)
```

1. **Cognitive Load Filter (W3C COGA):** The system hides background noise and displays only one primary action per screen, broken down into sequential micro-steps to offload the overwhelmed prefrontal cortex.
2. **Biologically Honest Approach (Anti-Punishment):** Gamification focused on truth and self-care. Reporting *"everything is bad"* scores the same as *"everything is fine"* (eliminating the symptom-hiding bias driven by fear of punishment). It utilizes flexible *streaks*: missing a day does not reset accumulated progress, avoiding amygdala threat triggers.
3. **Transition Buffers:** Smooths abrupt context shifts and sudden routine interruptions in confined environments, preserving focus and reducing crew frustration spikes.

## ⚙️ MVP Features and Components

The system operates through a high-performance web interface in a dark mode (*cyber-minimalist*), divided into two main integrated modules:

### 1. Presentation and Whitepaper Module (`Landing Page`)
* **Fluid Navigation (*Smooth Scroll*):** Dedicated sections detailing the space challenge, the cognitive prosthesis solution, architecture, and market differentiators.
* **Space Visual Identity:** Textured background with star particles (`space-field`), dynamic SVG orbits, and clean typography (*Inter* + *JetBrains Mono*).
* **Team Presentation:** Display of the researchers and engineers responsible for the project under the banner of the QUAS6R innovation team.

### 2. Active Application Module (`ISIS OS v0.1 — Core Local`)
* **Immediate State Check-in:** Interactive energy/fatigue sliders, sleep quality tracking, and real-time stress status.
* **Interactive 3D Body Mapping:** 
  * Three-dimensional visualizer based on a point cloud (managed via HTML5 Canvas).
  * Allows rotating, zooming, and selecting body zones (cardiovascular, neurological, musculoskeletal) to display instant local diagnoses and recommended action guidelines.
* **Biometric and Vital Signs Panel:** Autonomous offline monitoring of heart rate ($64\text{ bpm}$), accumulated radiation ($48.2\text{ mSv}$), and local network status (*Autonomous Mode*).

## 🚀 Projected Impact and Metrics in Orbit

* **100% reduction** in operational freezes (*decisional paralysis*) under high-pressure station conditions.
* **Engagement rates exceeding 90%** in multi-month missions utilizing the flexible retention system and streaks.
* **Active mitigation** of risks identified in NASA's medical gaps (**BMed-103 / BMed-108** concerning sleep deprivation and mental fatigue), with direct potential for terrestrial *spin-offs* for ADHD treatment and extreme environment management.
